# Nagless Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** 2026-08-21

Nagless does not collect, store, transmit, or sell any data. Period.

- **No analytics, no telemetry, no tracking.** The extension makes zero network requests.
- **No remote code.** Everything the extension runs ships inside the package reviewed by the store.
- **Local-only settings.** Your on/off state, per-site allowlist, and blocked-popup counters are stored in your browser's local extension storage (`storage.local` / `storage.session`) on your device. They never leave it.
- **Page access.** Nagless requires access to the pages you visit (`<all_urls>`) solely to detect and hide nag overlays in the page you are viewing. Page content is never read for any other purpose, never recorded, and never transmitted anywhere.

Questions: open an issue on this repository or email muhannad.dev@gmail.com.
